One-line: the only elevated surface in either system — reserve it for actions that move money or overwrite work.

```jsx
<Dialog
  eyebrow="Confirm"
  title="Trim Industrials to 12%?"
  footer={<><Button variant="ghost">Cancel</Button><Button variant="accent">Confirm trim</Button></>}
>
  This sells 640 shares across two names and moves the sector back inside its policy band.
</Dialog>
```

State the consequence in the body, not the title. The title asks; the body explains what will happen.
