# Output Templates

Clean, ATS-safe section templates by experience level and context. Copy structure; fill with real content.

---

## Contact Block (Universal)

```
FIRST LAST
City, State  |  (555) 555-5555  |  email@domain.com  |  linkedin.com/in/handle
```

No full street address. No photo. No DOB. No marital status.

## Summary / Profile

### When to include
- 5+ years of experience with a clear narrative
- Career pivot that needs explanation
- Executive level (VP+) — mandatory
- Skip if <3 years experience (bullets carry more weight)

### Template: IC / Mid-Senior

```
[Job Title] with [X] years of experience in [domain/industry]. 
[One sentence: what you're known for technically or functionally.]
[One sentence: a specific result, scope, or differentiator that wouldn't appear on every resume in this stack.]
```

**Filled example (Software Engineer):**
```
Backend engineer with 6 years building data infrastructure for fintech products.
Focused on high-throughput pipelines — most recently owning a system processing 4M+ events/day.
Currently seeking roles where data reliability and scale are the core technical challenge.
```

### Template: Executive (VP+)

```
[Level] [Function] leader with [X]+ years driving [type of outcome] in [industry/context].
[One sentence: leadership scope — team size, budget, P&L, geography.]
[One sentence: a specific business outcome at scale.]
[Optional: one line on leadership philosophy or approach, only if distinctive.]
```

## Experience Section

### Entry Format

```
JOB TITLE  |  Company Name  |  City, State (or Remote)  |  Month YYYY – Month YYYY

- [Strong verb] [what you did] [at what scale] [with what result]
- [Strong verb] [what you did] [at what scale] [with what result]
- [Strong verb] [what you did] [at what scale] [with what result]
- [Optional: collaborative/cross-functional bullet showing how you worked]
```

### Bullet count by role seniority

| Level | Bullets per role |
|---|---|
| Most recent role | 4–6 bullets |
| Prior role (2–3 years ago) | 3–4 bullets |
| Older roles (>5 years ago) | 2–3 bullets |
| Early career roles (>8 years ago) | 1–2 bullets or omit entirely |

**Pruning rule:** Every bullet cut from an old role creates space to expand the most recent and relevant one. Recency matters most to ATS and human readers.

## Certifications Section

```
CERTIFICATIONS

Certification Name  |  Issuing Body  |  Month Year (or "Expected Month Year" if in progress)
```

Expired certifications: only list if still widely recognized as a credential (e.g., CFA, CPA never expire in terms of the credential — the designation is maintained differently from exam recency). For time-limited tech certs (AWS, Google Cloud), list only if within renewal window.

## Cover Letter Structure (3 paragraphs max)

```
Paragraph 1 — Why this role, at this company
[One specific reason this role appeals — not generic "I've always admired X." One concrete thing: the product, the problem they're solving, the team, the market position. Show you did the research.]

Paragraph 2 — Why you, backed by one proof point
[The one achievement most directly relevant to the JD's core need. With a metric if possible. Not a restatement of the resume — a story behind a result.]

Paragraph 3 — What you bring + forward
[What you can do for them specifically. Then a clean, confident close — not "I hope to hear from you." Something like: "I'd welcome the chance to discuss how [specific thing] maps to what you're building."]
```

**Total length:** 200–300 words. A recruiter who opens a cover letter is looking for a reason to read it or a reason to stop. Make the first sentence do work.

## LinkedIn Adaptation Notes

LinkedIn profile ≠ resume. Key differences:

| Element | Resume | LinkedIn |
|---|---|---|
| Tone | Formal, terse | Warmer, slightly more conversational |
| Summary (About) | 3–5 lines max | Up to 2,600 chars — use fully if senior |
| Pronouns | None (implied first person) | First person "I" is standard |
| Bullets | 3–6 per role | 3–5 per role; shorter |
| Keyword density | Optimized for JD match | Optimized for recruiter search terms |
| Achievements | Highlighted | Same, but can link to media/portfolio |
| Skills section | Clean list | LinkedIn endorsements add social proof |

LinkedIn About section template:

```
[What you do in one sentence — clear, specific, not vague.]

[2–3 sentences on what you're known for, your approach, or a specific domain you've built expertise in. First person. Conversational but professional.]

[What you're working on now or what you're looking for — signals intent to recruiters.]

[Optional: where to connect or find more of your work.]
```
