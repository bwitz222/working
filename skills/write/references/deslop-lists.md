# Banned phrases

## Throat-clearing openers: cut, state the point
"Here's the thing:" | "Here's what/this/that/why [X]" | "The uncomfortable truth is" | "It turns out" | "The real [X] is" | "Let me be clear" | "The truth is," | "I'll say it again:" | "I'm going to be honest" | "Can we talk about" | "Here's what I find interesting" | "Here's the problem though"

## Emphasis crutches: delete entirely
"Full stop." | "Period." | "Let that sink in." | "This matters because" | "Make no mistake" | "Here's why that matters"

## Filler phrases: cut entirely
"At its core" | "In today's [X]" | "It's worth noting" | "At the end of the day" | "When it comes to" | "In a world where" | "The reality is"

## Adverbs: kill all -ly words
really | just | literally | genuinely | honestly | simply | actually | deeply | truly | fundamentally | inherently | inevitably | interestingly | importantly | crucially

## Business Jargon

| Avoid | Use instead |
|-------|-------------|
| Navigate (challenges) | Handle, address |
| Unpack | Explain, examine |
| Lean into | Accept, embrace |
| Landscape | Situation, field |
| Game-changer | Important, significant |
| Double down | Commit, increase |
| Deep dive | Analysis, examination |
| Take a step back | Reconsider |
| Moving forward | Next, from now |
| Circle back | Return to, revisit |
| On the same page | Aligned, agreed |

## Meta-commentary: remove, let the writing move
"Hint:" | "Plot twist:" | "Spoiler:" | "You already know this, but" | "But that's another post" | "X is a feature, not a bug" | "The rest of this explains..." | "Let me walk you through..." | "In this section, we'll..." | "As we'll see..." | "I want to explore..."

## Vague declaratives: replace with the specific thing
"The reasons are structural" | "The implications are significant" | "This is the deepest problem" | "The stakes are high" | "The consequences are real" | "This is genuinely hard" | "This is what X actually looks like" | "actually matters"

## Performative sincerity: cut
"creeps in" | "I promise" | "They exist, I promise"

# Structural patterns to avoid

## Binary Contrasts → State Y directly, drop the negation

| Avoid | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| "Not because X. Because Y." | "Because Y." |
| "[X] isn't the problem. [Y] is." | "[Y] is the problem." |
| "The answer isn't X. It's Y." | "The answer is Y." |
| "It feels like X. It's actually Y." | "It's Y." |
| "The question isn't X. It's Y." | State Y. |
| "Not X. But Y." / "not X, it's Y" | "Y." |
| "stops being X and starts being Y" | "becomes Y" or name who made it so |
| "not just X but also Y" | "X and Y" or just "Y" |

## Negative Listing → State Z, skip the runway

| Avoid | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| "Not a X... Not a Y... A Z." | "A Z." |
| "It wasn't X. It wasn't Y. It was Z." | "It was Z." |

## Dramatic Fragmentation → Complete sentences

| Avoid | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| "[Noun]. That's it. That's the [thing]." | One sentence. |
| "X. And Y. And Z." | "X, Y, and Z." |
| "This unlocks something. [Word]." | Name the thing it unlocks. |

## Rhetorical Setups → Make the point directly

| Avoid | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| "What if [reframe]?" | State the reframe. |
| "Here's what I mean:" | Cut. Next sentence is what you mean. |
| "Think about it:" | Cut. |
| "And that's okay." | Cut. |

## False Agency → Name the human

AI defaults to inanimate subjects doing human things. Name who actually did it.

| Avoid | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| "a complaint becomes a fix" | "the team fixed it" |
| "a bet lives or dies in days" | "someone kills the project or ships it" |
| "the decision emerges" | "someone decides" |
| "the culture shifts" | "people change behavior" |
| "the data tells us" | "the analysis shows" / "Chen read the data and concluded" |
| "the market rewards" | "buyers pay for" |

If no specific person fits, use "you" to put the reader in the seat.

## Narrator-from-a-Distance → Put reader in the room

| Avoid | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| "Nobody designed this." | "You didn't sit down and decide to..." |
| "People tend to..." | "You..." |
| "This happens because..." | Name the specific cause. |

## Passive Voice → Find the actor

| Avoid | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| "X was created" | Name who created it |
| "It is believed that" | Name who believes it |
| "Mistakes were made" | Name who made them |
| "The decision was reached" | Name who decided |

## Sentence Starters to Restructure

| Avoid | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| What / When / Where / Which / Who / Why / How openers | Lead with subject or verb |
| Paragraphs starting with "So" | Start with content |
| "Look," | Remove |

## Rhythm Traps

| Pattern | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Three-item lists | Cut to two (or one) |
| Every paragraph ends punchily | Vary endings |
| Em dashes | Remove. Use commas or periods |
| Staccato stack (3+ short sentences) | Merge or restructure |
| "Not always. Not perfectly." | Cut hedging disguised as reassurance |
