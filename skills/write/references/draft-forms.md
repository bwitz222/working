# Draft forms

Form conventions. This file carries the business, investor, and client forms plus the rules that
apply to every form. Teaching, essay, and creative forms live in `draft-forms-long.md`; load that
one instead when the piece is a lecture, a lesson plan, an essay, an op-ed, or narrative work.
Load one form section, not all of them.

## Contents
- Business and investor
- Client communication
- Cross-form rules
- Elsewhere: teaching, general prose, creative and narrative, in `draft-forms-long.md`

## Business and investor
Memos, board books, investor letters, MD&A, fund and portfolio commentary, proposals, IC
materials, internal reports, executive summaries.

**Intake nuances** (ask only if not inferable)
- Decision or record? Recommending vs. documenting. Changes the spine entirely.
- Reader's altitude. Board or principal vs. peer vs. direct report. Higher altitude means
  lead with the conclusion and compress the how.
- External exposure. Anything going to board, investors, lenders, regulators, clients, or
  publication triggers the human-review flag and any org editorial or brand skill.

**Structure defaults**
- BLUF. The ask, recommendation, or headline lands in the first two sentences. Executives
  read the top and the numbers. Inverted pyramid after that: conclusion, support, detail,
  appendix. Let readers stop early, one idea per paragraph, lead sentence carrying it.
- Memo skeleton: purpose line, brief context, recommendation or findings, rationale, next
  steps and asks. Use the org template skill to produce the file itself.
- Numbers persuade. A specific figure beats an adjective; comparatives carry context (vs.
  prior period, plan, benchmark). Structure financial data for spreadsheet import.

**Voice and register interaction.** Formal-neutral to professional-warm. Confident, not
breathless. No hype adjectives ("transformative," "game-changing," "robust"). Active voice,
named actors. Hedge only where uncertainty is genuine, and quantify it. Business suppresses
the soul check: no first person singular, no reactions, no rhythmic flourish in board or
investor copy. First person plural is allowed, per naturalness dial setting 2.
GATE v1 here removes tells (jargon, false agency, inflation, hedging mush) while keeping the
surface neutral. Run the pattern scan, skip the soul check. Plain and precise beats lively.

**Org style skills win.** If an organizational editorial, brand, or template skill applies
to the deliverable, its rules outrank the generic choices here. Apply it to downloadable
files. Current org skills: purdue-smif-editorial-style, purdue-smif-brand-guidelines, and
purdue-smif-design.

**Anti-patterns.** Throat-clearing before the ask · "I wanted to reach out" ·
synergy/leverage/circle-back jargon · passive accountability ("mistakes were made") ·
adjective stacks standing in for evidence.

## Client communication
Client emails, quarterly and annual review letters, meeting recaps, allocation change notes,
market-event and drawdown notes, onboarding and IPS documents, fee conversations, prospect
one-pagers. Load `domain-wealth.md` with this section, always. It is not optional and its
compliance gate is blocking.

**Intake nuances** (ask only if not inferable)
- One household or many? A note to one client can reference their circumstances. A note to the
  book cannot, and every recommendation in it is general by construction.
- What triggered it? A scheduled review, a market move, a client question, a transaction, or a
  problem. The trigger sets the first sentence, and getting it wrong is the most common failure
  in this form.
- What does the client have to do? Nothing, sign something, decide something, or call. The
  answer belongs at the end and shapes everything before it.
- Emotional temperature. A drawdown note and a rebalancing note are the same form under
  different pressure and require different first paragraphs.

**Structure defaults**
- Answer their question in the first two sentences. In a selloff the question is "am I going to
  be fine," not "what is your macro view." A note that opens with context has already lost.
- One idea per message. A performance update plus a recommendation plus a paperwork request
  produces a reply to the paperwork request only.
- Email specifically: subject line states the substance, not the category. "Trimming healthcare,
  need your OK by Friday" beats "Portfolio update." Front-load, because it is read on a phone.
  Under 300 words in the body; anything longer is an attachment with a three-line cover note.
- Quarterly letter: what happened, what it meant for this household, what changed in the
  portfolio and why, what happens next. Not a market recap with the portfolio bolted on.
- Close with the next step, its owner, and its date. Including "nothing needed from you."

**Voice and register interaction.** Naturalness dial 2. Warm, plain, and specific, with no
jargon left untranslated on first use. Institutional enough that the compliance language does
not read as an intrusion, human enough that the client finishes it. Run the pattern scan, skip
the soul check. Confidence is stated as confidence and uncertainty as uncertainty; performed
certainty in this form is a liability in both senses.

**Anti-patterns.** Reassurance built on adjectives rather than the plan · "there is no reason for
concern" · "as you know" in front of something they do not know · a market narrative standing in
for a portfolio answer · disclosure dropped mid-paragraph where it reads as an admission ·
hedging heavy enough that the recommendation disappears · a number with no as-of date.

## Cross-form rules

| Rule | Applies to |
|---|---|
| Missing fact becomes a visible [TK: ...] placeholder | all forms |
| No invented figures, sources, quotes, attributions; no figure or proper noun altered | all forms |
| Human-review flag on board, investor, lender, regulator, client, or publication exposure | all forms |
| Soul check: suppressed / full / with judgment | business, client, teaching / prose / creative |
| Load `domain-wealth.md`, compliance gate blocking | client communication |
| Load `domain-finance.md`, compliance gate blocking | any piece touching a ticker, portfolio, client, or figure |
| Load `domain-notes.md` | teaching, academic, persuasive |
| Gate the extracted text of the built file, not only the draft | any docx, pptx, or xlsx deliverable |
